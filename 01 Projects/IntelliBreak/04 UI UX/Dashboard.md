### What I like

- Sidebar navigation

- Clear visual hierarchy

### What I don't like

- Too many colors
- Excessive rounded cards
- Too much information above the fold

### Apply to IntelliBreak

- Use 12-column grid
- 24–32px outer margin
- 16–24px gutters
- Keep dashboard relatively clean
