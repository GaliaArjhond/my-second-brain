# Thesis Chapter 1 and 2

CHAPTER 1

PROJECT RATIONALE

Introduction

 Technology has changed the way people work, especially with the rise of remote work and work-from-home (WFH) jobs. Today, many people work as Virtual Assistants (VAs), freelancers, and other online professionals who complete their tasks from home using a computer and an internet connection. This type of work offers many advantages, such as flexible schedules, reduced travel time, and the opportunity to work for clients from different parts of the world. However, despite these benefits, working from home also brings challenges that can affect both productivity and personal well-being.

 One of the biggest challenges faced by Virtual Assistants and Work-from-Home professionals is maintaining a healthy balance between work and personal life. Since they usually work from home without direct supervision, many find it difficult to separate working hours from personal time. Some workers become so focused on finishing their tasks that they forget to take breaks, skip meals, or continue working beyond their regular schedule. Over time, these habits can lead to stress, tiredness, burnout, and lower productivity. On the other hand, some workers may struggle to stay focused because of distractions at home, causing delays in completing their tasks and affecting their work performance.

 Being productive is not only about finishing more work. It is also about taking care of one's health while working. Regular breaks, proper mealtimes, and enough rest are important in helping workers stay focused, motivated, and productive throughout the day. However, many remote workers become so busy with their responsibilities that they forget to follow these healthy habits. Because of this, there is a growing need for a system that can help users manage both their work and their well-being.

 With the help of machine learning, computer systems can now learn from users' daily activities and identify patterns in their work habits. By analyzing information such as working hours, completed tasks, break schedules, lunch times, and inactive periods, a machine learning system can provide recommendations based on each user's behavior. Instead of giving the same reminder to everyone, the system can offer suggestions that match the user's actual work routine, making the recommendations more useful and meaningful.

 To address these concerns, this study proposes IntelliBreak: A Machine Learning-Based Productivity and Work-Life Balance Recommendation System. IntelliBreak is a web-based application designed specifically for Virtual Assistants and Work-from-Home professionals. The system allows users to manage their daily tasks while monitoring their work habits. By using machine learning, IntelliBreak can identify whether a user is working for too long without taking a break or becoming less productive during working hours. Based on these observations, the system provides personalized recommendations that encourage users to maintain a healthy balance between productivity and personal well-being. Through this approach, IntelliBreak aims to help remote workers build healthier work habits, reduce the risk of burnout, and improve their overall work-life balance.

Research Gap

 Many productivity applications, such as Habitica, Todoist, and Trello, are designed to help users organize their tasks, manage deadlines, and improve productivity. These applications are useful for keeping track of daily work and encouraging users to complete their responsibilities. Some also include features like reminders, progress tracking, and gamification to make task management more engaging.

 However, these applications mainly focus on helping users finish their tasks and do not fully support their work-life balance. Most of them cannot monitor whether users are working continuously without taking breaks, skipping lunch, working beyond healthy hours, or becoming inactive during their scheduled work time. In addition, the reminders provided by these applications are usually fixed and are not based on the user's actual working habits.

 For Virtual Assistants and Work-from-Home professionals, maintaining a healthy work-life balance is especially important because they often work independently and have flexible schedules. Their work routines may change every day depending on their workload and clients' demands. As a result, they need a system that not only helps them manage tasks but also understands their work behavior and provides recommendations based on their daily activities.

 This study aims to address these gaps by developing IntelliBreak. Unlike existing productivity applications, IntelliBreak combines task management with machine learning to analyze users' work patterns, including their working hours, completed tasks, break schedules, lunch times, and inactive periods. Based on these patterns, the system provides personalized recommendations that encourage healthier work habits while helping users remain productive. By focusing on both productivity and well-being, IntelliBreak offers a more balanced approach that meets the needs of Virtual Assistants and Work-from-Home professionals.

Work-Life Balance Among Remote Workers

 Working from home has changed the way many people perform their daily jobs. According to Tejero, Seva, and Fadrilan-Camacho (2021), many Filipino employees experienced difficulty maintaining a good work-life balance because work and family responsibilities often happened at the same time. Since they worked from home, many employees found it difficult to separate work from personal life. The study also found that long working hours, heavy workloads, and stress affect both their health and productivity.

 This literature is related to the present study because IntelliBreak is designed to help Virtual Assistants and Work-from-Home professionals develop healthier work habits. By monitoring their working hours and break schedules, the system can provide recommendations that encourage users to maintain a better balance between work and personal life.

Productivity While Working from Home

 Productivity is one of the biggest concerns for people working remotely. Seva et al. (2021) explained that the productivity of Filipino employees working from home depends on different factors, such as stress, the work environment, available equipment, and how well they manage their time. The study found that employees who experienced less stress were generally more productive than those who experienced high levels of stress.

 This literature supports the proposed study because IntelliBreak helps users monitor their work habits and encourages them to take regular breaks and manage their workload properly. These recommendations may help users stay productive while also taking care of their health.

Work-Life Balance and Employee Well-Being

 Caballero (2022) explained that working from home gives employees more freedom, but it also makes it harder to separate work from personal life. Many employees continue working even after their regular working hours because they no longer have clear boundaries between work and home. The study also found that employees who maintain a healthy work-life balance are generally happier, more motivated, and more productive.

 This literature supports the goal of IntelliBreak because the proposed system encourages users to develop healthy work habits instead of focusing only on completing tasks. It reminds users to take breaks and manage their working hours properly to avoid stress and burning out.

The researchers suggested that technology can help employees better manage their daily work and improve their overall well-being.

Healthy Work Habits

 According to Caringal-Go et al. (2021), employees who practice good time management and healthy work habits are more likely to maintain a good work-life balance. The study showed that taking regular breaks, setting a fixed work schedule, creating a proper workspace, and communicating with family members and co-workers helped reduce stress and improve productivity.

 This literature is related to the proposed study because IntelliBreak encourages users to follow these healthy habits by monitoring their work activities and providing recommendations that match their daily work routine.

General Objective

 The main objective of this study is to develop IntelliBreak: A Machine Learning-Based Productivity and Work-Life Balance Recommendation System, a web-based application that helps Virtual Assistants and Work-from-Home (WFH) professionals become more productive while maintaining a healthy work-life balance. The system will monitor users' work habits, analyze their daily activities using machine learning, and provide personalized recommendations that encourage healthy working routines and help prevent burnout.

Specific Objectives

This study aims to:

Design and develop a web-based productivity and work-life balance recommendation system for Virtual Assistants and Work-from-Home professionals.

Allow users to create, organize, update, and complete their daily tasks using a checklist.

Monitor users' working hours, completed tasks, break times, lunch schedules, and inactive periods.

Use machine learning to analyze users' work habits and identify patterns of overworking or underworking.

Provide personalized recommendations that help users maintain a healthy balance between work and personal life.

Display users' productivity, work history, completed tasks, and recommendations through a simple dashboard.

Evaluate the system based on the ISO/IEC 25010 Software Quality Model in terms of functionality, usability, reliability, performance efficiency, security, maintainability, portability, and compatibility.

Scope of the Study

 This study focuses on the design and development of IntelliBreak: A Machine Learning-Based Productivity and Work-Life Balance Recommendation System, a web-based application created for Virtual Assistants and Work-from-Home (WFH) professionals.

 The system allows users to manage their daily tasks through a checklist and monitor their work activities, such as working hours, break times, lunch schedules, completed tasks, and inactive periods. Using machine learning, the system analyzes these work habits and provides personalized recommendations to help users maintain a healthy balance between productivity and personal well-being.

 The system also includes user registration, login, task management, productivity monitoring, work history, recommendations, and a dashboard where users can view their work progress and productivity reports. The application can be accessed through a web browser using a desktop or laptop computer with an internet connection.

Delimitations of the Study

This study has the following limitations:

The system is intended only for Virtual Assistants and Work-from-Home professionals.

The application is web-based and will not include a mobile application.

The system only monitors information recorded within the application, such as working hours, completed tasks, break times, lunch schedules, and inactive periods.

Recommendations are based only on the information collected by the system and the machine learning model.

The system is designed to provide recommendations only and will not replace the performance evaluation conducted by employers or clients.

The application does not include payroll, attendance monitoring, client communication, video conferencing, or project management features.

The study will evaluate the system using the ISO/IEC 25010 Software Quality Model.

Theoretical Framework

 This study is based on the Work-Life Balance Theory, Self-Determination Theory, and the concept of Machine Learning Recommendation Systems.

 The Work-Life Balance Theory explains that people perform better when they maintain a healthy balance between work and personal life. Working for long hours without enough rest can lead to stress, tiredness, and burnout, while taking regular breaks and managing time properly can improve both health and productivity. This theory supports the purpose of IntelliBreak because the system encourages users to develop healthy work habits by providing reminders and recommendations based on their daily work activities.

 The study is also supported by the Self-Determination Theory, developed by Edward Deci and Richard Ryan. This theory explains that people become more motivated and productive when they have control over their own work and are able to make their own decisions. Instead of forcing users to follow a fixed schedule, IntelliBreak provides recommendations that help users improve their work habits while allowing them to decide how they will manage their time.

 Another foundation of this study is the concept of Machine Learning Recommendation Systems. Machine learning allows a computer system to learn from users' previous activities and identify patterns in their behavior. In IntelliBreak, the system analyzes information such as working hours, completed tasks, break times, lunch schedules, and inactive periods. Based on these patterns, the system provides personalized recommendations that encourage users to take breaks, avoid working for too many hours, and maintain a healthy balance between work and personal life.

 These theories and concepts serve as the foundation for developing IntelliBreak: A Machine Learning-Based Productivity and Work-Life Balance Recommendation System. They support the goal of helping Virtual Assistants and Work-from-Home professionals improve their productivity while maintaining healthy work habits and a better work-life balance.
